About Me

Hi, I'm Igo! I work in after-sales service operations in the automotive industry, where my job is to help a network of service outlets reach their targets — with data. I monitor KPIs, work out which outlets need attention and why, and coach outlet managers with the numbers in hand. I studied Geophysical Engineering, where analysing field data was the core of the work, and carried that habit into operations, building my own Power BI dashboards and Excel models along the way. I'm now completing the Full-Stack Data Analytics programme at RevoU to put a formal foundation under that experience, and I'm looking for a Data Analyst role.
